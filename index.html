I am a machine learning researcher and software engineer specializing in efficient LLM inference, numerical optimization, and reliable AI systems. At Universitaet Trier, I research KV cache compression under Prof. Dr. Volker Schulz, pairing graduate research with more than four years of enterprise software development experience at SAP Labs India.

My primary research tackles the memory wall in long-context language models. Through JoLT, an approach combining partial Tucker decomposition with low-bit residuals, I demonstrated near-lossless 2x to 3x KV cache compression across five models—maintaining accuracy within 2 percentage points on 64K-context and GSM8K benchmarks, with a custom fused kernel reducing peak decode memory by 3.61x. I also study model trust, exploring whether step-by-step explanations reflect actual model reasoning and analyzing how asymmetric penalties in RLVR steer model calibration.

Having designed large-scale distributed systems and compliance workflows at SAP, I hold machine learning to high engineering standards. I build complete systems—including a production RAG assistant on Google Cloud Run and a sub-100ms Two-Tower recommender on Azure—focused on rigorous empirical validation, measured performance, and transparent limitations.

Tensor Decomposition of Transformer Key-Value Caches: Spectral Structure and Format Comparison

Rahul Krishnan and Volker Schulz, Universitaet Trier

arXiv preprint, in preparation for Numerical Linear Algebra with Applications (NLAA), 2026

A companion to the JoLT paper that treats the KV cache as a fourth-order tensor, measures its singular-value spectra on Mistral-7B-v0.3 and LLaMA-2-13B, and compares Tucker, CP, tensor train and t-SVD at matched storage.

Result

Token and feature modes are low-rank while head and layer modes are nearly full-rank. Tucker has the lowest reconstruction error at every compression ratio from 2x to 5x, and post-RoPE keys lose 41% to 64% of their pre-RoPE compressibility.

A JoLT for the KV Cache

Rahul Krishnan and Volker Schulz, Universitaet Trier

arXiv preprint, 2026

My MSc thesis preprint reports near-lossless 2x to 3x KV cache compression, with accuracy staying within 2 percentage points of the uncompressed model, across five models and four model families.

Result
